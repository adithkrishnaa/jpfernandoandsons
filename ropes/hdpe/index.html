HDPE rope

Polyethylene rope in coils and many colours, including our SuperCatch label.

HDPE (high-density polyethylene) rope is a light, practical rope used widely across fishing and marine work.

Our polyethylene ropes are supplied in coils in a range of sizes and colours — including blue, green, red, yellow and white — many under our own SuperCatch label.
