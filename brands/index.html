A history of agencies

Agent for world-renowned brands

Over the decades J.P. Fernando & Sons became importing and distributing agent for Nichimen Corporation, Japan, Miracle Brand, Japan and Namayang Corporation, Korea — for many years among the most popular and leading brands in the island.

Since the mid-1990s, as customers became more price-sensitive and production costs rose in Japan and Korea, we have also distributed many leading Indian fishing-net brands, while manufacturing our own brands locally.
