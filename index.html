Our story

Sri Lanka's first distributor of imported fishing nets.

J.P. Fernando & Sons was founded in 1953 to bring imported fishing nets and accessories to Sri Lankan fishermen. Through decades as agent for Japanese and Korean brands, and later as a manufacturer in our own right, one principle has stayed the same: Quality First.

The range

Everything a fishing boat needs, from net to hook.

Seven product families, supplied to wholesale buyers, government tenders and individual fishermen alike.

  • 01 Nylon Fishing Nets Our own SuperCatch nets, knitted locally from TORAY Japan yarn, alongside imported Japanese-technology nets.
  • 02 HDPE Fishing Nets Strong, hard-wearing high-density polyethylene netting in green, blue and white — made to your requirements.
  • 03 Fishing Lines Monofilament line in a range of diameters and colours — from clear to fluorescent.
  • 04 Ropes HDPE, PP and Vinylon ropes in a range of sizes and colours, including our own SuperCatch rope.
  • 05 Fishing Tackles & Swivels Hooks, lures, sinkers, floats and swivels — the working kit of every angler.
  • 06 Fishing Accessories Floats, cast nets, reels, rods and diving gear — the everyday items that keep fishing going.
  • 07 Nylon Yarn & Twine TORAY Japan nylon yarn — we are the sole agent and single distributor for TORAY products in Sri Lanka.
